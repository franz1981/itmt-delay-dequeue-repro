  Wei Yang, "[PATCH v4] sched/stats: Fix run_delay over-count for migrated
  sched_delayed tasks", 2026-10-24, not merged in 7.4-rc1
  https://lore.kernel.org/all/20261024042753.2276779-1-albin_yang@163.com/
  A sched_delayed task migrated by active balance or move_queued_task gets
  the sleep between migration and wakeup added to run_delay. The repro's
  wait% is run_delay from /proc/thread-self/schedstat, and the traces show
  active-balance migrations, so wait% on 7.1.13 can include that over-count.
  stacked / handoff_max_ms are user-space timestamps and are not affected.
  Size of the over-count in this test: not measured. (Inference, not
  measured: the workers sleep ~10 us per cycle, so each such event would
  add about that much, small next to 1 ms stalls.)
